Report, Track, and Resolve Maintenance Issues with Ease

Our mobile app provides a convenient and efficient way for residents to report and track maintenance issues, access society information, and receive guidance on rules and procedures.

User Registration

Residents can register and create a profile to access the app's features

Complaint Reporting

Residents can report maintenance issues with details and photos

Real-time Tracking

AI-powered Guide

Residents can access a guide that provides instant answers to questions about society rules, amenities, and procedures

Push Notifications

Residents receive push notifications for updates on their reported complaints and important society announcements
